About the programme

What is CDWF?

Climate Dialogues: Way Forward (CDWF) is an outreach programme designed to raise awareness about climate science, climate change, and related issues by exploring them in depth - yet in a language everyone can follow.

The series is a one-hour live event conducted via Zoom, held every 3rd or 4th Monday of each month at approximately 6-7 pm IST. Every session is free from technical jargon and aims to reach students of Assam and inspire them to pursue climate research.

From tropical cyclones to AI in climate science, from paleo-climate records to sustainable cities - CDWF brings frontier research to local living rooms.
